## The problem

Every small team I work with ends up paying for a scheduling tool long before scheduling is the thing that
makes them money.

## What CalendaVibe does

- Booking links connected to **Google Calendar** and **Outlook**.
- **Google Meet** links created automatically for every booking.
- **Stripe** payments for paid sessions.
- **Teams**, so a booking can land on whoever is free.

## How it is built

React on the front end, Node.js and PostgreSQL behind it.
