I've spent 12+ years building and scaling software in banking, fintech, insurance and e-commerce. Almost
everything I know I learned by making decisions with incomplete information, in front of a team, a client or a
dashboard gone red. This blog is where I'm going to write it down.

## What you'll find here

- **CTO Log.** What really happens in the CTO seat: architecture decisions, hiring, incidents, roadmaps that
  change mid-quarter, and what I'd do differently.
- **Launches.** The products I build or lead, why they exist and what problem they solve.
- **Signals.** Industry news, read with one question in mind: what does this mean for a startup or an SME
  that has to decide with a small team and little time?

## Who I'm writing for

Founders who have to make technical decisions without being technical, product teams growing faster than
their architecture, and other CTOs who want to compare notes.

## How I'll write it

Short, concrete, no fluff. If there's a number, it's one I can stand behind. If something went wrong, I'll say
so. And I won't publish anything confidential about the clients I work with: when I talk about a case, it will
be with their permission or abstracted enough that it can't be recognised.

I write in English and Spanish. If something here is useful to you, or you disagree, message me on LinkedIn
or book a call: the best ideas for a post always come out of a conversation.
